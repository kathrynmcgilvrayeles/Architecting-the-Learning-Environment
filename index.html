The intent

Capability, then confidence

A quiz score proves recall. It does not prove a person can act under real pressure.

Understanding remains a critical foundation of any learning experience. But we have to know exactly which skill is being built, then deliberately design for the learner to apply it afterwards, in the messy reality of the job.

If the learning ends at the assessment, we have only designed half the experience. The other half is execution.
